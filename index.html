How did the Universe begin? This question has occupied humanity for millenia. Whilst we have not yet answered this question, modern cosmology has taken us closer to it. This website describes how scientists working in the Simons Observatory collaboration are trying to advance our understanding of this fascinating area!

The Simons Observatory is going on tour! From the 30th of June 2026 to the 5th of July 2026 the Simons Observatory will be at the Royal Society's Summer Science Exhibition. Please return nearer the event to find more details on these ideas!
